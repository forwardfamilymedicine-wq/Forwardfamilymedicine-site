In May, I wrote that Forward Family Medicine was [coming to Chester County](/blog/welcoming-dr-brian-landi/). Our Malvern office is now open at 301 Lindenwood Dr #206, and Dr. Brian Landi is seeing patients there.

Dr. Landi spent September meeting prospective patients in the new space, and in October he began seeing members for care. If you have been waiting for an office closer to home than Wayne, this is it.

## What the Malvern office offers

Malvern runs on the same membership as Wayne. That means visits long enough to actually talk through what is going on, typically one to two hours for a new patient. It means reaching your physician directly by phone, text, or video when you need to, rather than through a call center. It means a panel capped at roughly 250 patients, so your doctor knows you. And it means a flat monthly membership in place of insurance billing for routine care. You keep your insurance for specialists, hospitals, and imaging; the membership covers your relationship with your primary care doctor.

Dr. Landi is board-certified in Family Medicine and a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner. For women navigating perimenopause or menopause, that means the care can come from your own primary care physician, inside your membership, without a separate specialist referral. Our [menopause care page](/menopause/) explains what that includes.

Many follow-ups work well by phone or video, so you will not need to drive in for every question.

## Who it serves

The office sits along the Route 202 and Route 30 corridors, which makes it a practical option for families in Malvern, Paoli, Exton, Downingtown, Chester Springs, Phoenixville, and the surrounding Chester County communities.
