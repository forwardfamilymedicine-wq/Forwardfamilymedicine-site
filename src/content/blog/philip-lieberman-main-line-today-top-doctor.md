## Family medicine with lifestyle medicine built in

Dr. Lieberman is board-certified in both family medicine and lifestyle medicine, and he practices lifestyle medicine as part of everyday primary care. [Lifestyle medicine](/lifestyle/lifestyle-medicine/what-is-lifestyle-medicine/) is the evidence-based use of nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management, social connection, and avoiding risky substances to prevent and treat chronic disease.

At Forward Family Medicine, that is not a separate program or a lecture tacked onto the end of a visit. It is part of how the problems people come in with get approached: blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, weight, sleep, energy. A plan might include a medication. It might include a specific change to how someone eats or moves. Often it includes both, and the point is to choose what will actually work for that person.

Lifestyle medicine does not replace indicated medications, screening, or specialist care. It works alongside them.

## Starting with the person

Good lifestyle advice depends on the details of someone's life. What do they eat on a busy weeknight? How much do they sleep? What has worked before, and what has not? What do they actually want from their health in five or ten years?

Those questions take time, and the direct primary care model gives Dr. Lieberman that time. New-patient visits can run one to two hours, and patients can reach him directly between visits. Continuity matters just as much: a plan gets revisited as life changes, instead of being repeated the same way at every appointment.
