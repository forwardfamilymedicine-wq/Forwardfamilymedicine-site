Main Line Today has named me one of its Top Doctors for 2026. My colleague [Dr. Philip Lieberman](/blog/philip-lieberman-main-line-today-top-doctor/) was named as well, which made the news better.

I would rather use the occasion to talk about what the recognition reflects than about the recognition itself. It reflects a practice, and a practice is a lot of people.

## Why I started Forward Family Medicine

I trained as an osteopathic family physician, at the Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and then in residency at Christiana Care. That training pushes you to look at the whole person: the history, the habits, the family, the work, and what a patient is actually worried about when they walk in.

I opened Forward Family Medicine in Wayne in 2024 to practice that way every day. The direct primary care model is what makes it possible. Instead of billing insurance visit by visit, patients pay a flat monthly membership, and that changes the shape of the day. New-patient visits can run one to two hours. Patients reach their physician directly, by phone, text, or video, rather than through a call center. Panels stay small, so the doctor knows who you are when you call.

That structure, more than any one doctor, is what makes the practice work.

## Prevention and the whole picture

Much of what I care about in medicine happens before anything goes wrong. That means understanding cardiovascular risk properly, finding problems early, and treating sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress as part of medical care rather than an afterthought. A longer visit makes room for those conversations. Continuity makes it possible to follow through on them over years, instead of starting from scratch with a new face each time.

## From one doctor to a team

When I opened the practice, it was me. It is now three physicians and two offices. Dr. Philip Lieberman practices family medicine and lifestyle medicine at our Wayne office. Dr. Brian Landi practices family medicine at our Malvern office and is a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner. Ashley Arocho keeps the practice running day to day.

Recognition like this arrives one name at a time. The care our patients receive comes from all of them.
