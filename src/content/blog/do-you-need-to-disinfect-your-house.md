## Cleaning vs. Disinfecting

Cleaning removes dirt and many germs from surfaces. Disinfecting uses a chemical or other method to kill many of the remaining germs.

For most everyday household situations, routine cleaning is enough.

## Your Home Does Not Need to Be Sterile

Humans live alongside enormous communities of microorganisms. The goal of a healthy home is not to eliminate every microbe.

Overusing disinfectants can also increase unnecessary chemical exposure, particularly when products are sprayed or used repeatedly in poorly ventilated spaces.

## When Disinfection Makes Sense

Disinfection is useful when someone in the household is sick with an infectious illness, after certain types of contamination, in settings with specific infection-control recommendations, or when a healthcare professional or public-health guidance recommends it.

Outside those situations, routine cleaning is often sufficient.

## What About Children?

Children spend a great deal of time touching floors, surfaces, toys, and other objects. You do not need to disinfect every surface they encounter.

Focus on regular cleaning of high-touch and visibly dirty surfaces, and use disinfection intentionally when it is actually warranted.

## Think About Fragrance, Too

Many cleaning products contain fragrances that are not necessary for cleaning.

If you want to reduce unnecessary chemical exposure, consider fragrance-free products and avoid using air fresheners simply to make a room smell different.

Clean air is more important than a particular scent.

## A Simple Cleaning Strategy

For most homes:

- Clean regularly.
- Use soap and water when appropriate.
- Ventilate when using cleaning products.
- Follow product directions.
- Disinfect when there is a specific reason to do so.
- Keep cleaning products safely away from children.

## The Bottom Line

You do not need to sterilize your house to have a healthy home.

Cleanliness matters. Hygiene matters. Infection prevention matters.

But more is not always better.

A sensible home environment is one where you clean consistently, disinfect intentionally, and avoid unnecessary chemical exposure whenever practical.
