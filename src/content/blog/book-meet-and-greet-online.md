Until recently, setting up a meet and greet with one of our physicians meant a phone call or a few messages back and forth to find a time that worked. You can now book one yourself, online, whenever it suits you.

## What a meet and greet is

A meet and greet is a free conversation with the physician who would be your doctor. There is no cost and no obligation to join. It is a chance to ask how the membership works, what a first visit looks like, how you reach your doctor between visits, and whether our way of practicing fits what you are looking for.

Most people come with a few questions. Useful ones include how urgent concerns are handled after hours, how referrals to specialists work, what the membership includes and what it does not, and what insurance you may still want to keep. Our post on [choosing a primary care doctor on the Main Line](/blog/choosing-primary-care-doctor-main-line/) has a longer list.

## How booking works

Start with the short [meet and greet form](/contact/) on our website. After you submit it, you choose the physician you would like to meet and see their open times. Pick the one that works for you and you will receive a confirmation.

You can meet Dr. Philip Lieberman, MD, at our Wayne office at 295 Old Eagle School Road, Suite 2, or Dr. Brian Landi, DO, at our Malvern office at 301 Lindenwood Dr #206. Dr. Bigley's panel is full, so meet and greets are with Dr. Lieberman and Dr. Landi.

## Choosing between Dr. Lieberman and Dr. Landi

For most people, location is the simplest guide. Wayne is closer for much of the Main Line, and Malvern is closer for much of Chester County.

If you have a particular reason for coming in, that can help too. Dr. Lieberman's work combines family medicine with lifestyle medicine, so he is a natural fit if nutrition, exercise, weight, or metabolic health is high on your list. Dr. Landi is a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner, so he is a natural fit if perimenopause or menopause care is part of why you are looking. Both are family physicians who provide the full range of primary care, and you can read more about each of them on our [team page](/meet-the-team/).
