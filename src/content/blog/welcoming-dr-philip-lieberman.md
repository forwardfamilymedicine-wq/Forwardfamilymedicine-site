When I started Forward Family Medicine in September 2024, my goal was simple: build a practice where patients are actually known by their physician. Where visits are an hour, not seven minutes. Where lifestyle, nutrition, sleep, and stress get the same clinical attention as labs and prescriptions.

That vision needed a second physician who shared it. I'm thrilled to share that we've found him.

## Meet Dr. Philip Lieberman

Dr. Philip Lieberman, MD, joins Forward Family Medicine this month at our Wayne, PA office. He is dual board-eligible in Family Medicine and Lifestyle Medicine — and his path to medicine is unlike any physician you've met.

Before medical school, Dr. Lieberman earned an Associate's Degree in Culinary Arts and a Bachelor of Science in Nutrition Science from Pennsylvania State University. That foundation in food, metabolism, and lifestyle is the kind of training most physicians never receive. He went on to earn his Doctor of Medicine from Thomas Jefferson University in Philadelphia, served as a primary care physician in the U.S. Navy, and completed residency training at Abington Family Medicine — where he didn't just learn lifestyle medicine, he developed a comprehensive curriculum that enabled fellow residents to qualify for board certification through the American College of Lifestyle Medicine.

In other words: the man teaches the curriculum.

## Why Lifestyle Medicine matters here

Forward Family Medicine has always been more than just primary care without insurance. It's a practice built around the principle that most chronic disease — high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, metabolic dysfunction, cardiovascular risk — responds better to nutrition, movement, sleep, and stress reduction than to another prescription. Dr. Lieberman doesn't just believe this; he has the clinical training, the culinary background, and the curriculum-development experience to deliver it in a way that actually works in real life.

For our patients, that means the lifestyle conversations that other practices skip get the time and depth they deserve. It means nutrition advice that's specific, practical, and grounded in what you'll actually cook. It means a physician who treats the whole picture, not just the lab values.

## What this means if you're a current patient

Nothing changes about your care with me. My panel remains capped at the ~250-patient model that makes this practice work. Dr. Lieberman opens his own panel as he joins, which means we can welcome more new families into the practice without compromising the experience that makes Forward Family Medicine different.

If you've been thinking about a friend or family member who might benefit from this kind of care — someone navigating a chronic condition, someone tired of feeling rushed at every appointment, someone who wants their physician to actually know them — Dr. Lieberman is now accepting new members.
