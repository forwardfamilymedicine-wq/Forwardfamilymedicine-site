These four articles follow the menopause transition from its earliest signs through the decade after the final period. They are written to be read in order, but each one stands on its own.

## The articles

**[Perimenopause in Your Early Forties: The Symptoms Nobody Connects](/lifestyle/menopause/perimenopause-early-forties/)**

Perimenopause often begins years before periods change, and its earliest symptoms rarely get linked together. What to watch for, why hormone tests mislead, and when to have the conversation.

**[What a Menopause Visit Should Actually Cover](/lifestyle/menopause/what-a-menopause-visit-should-cover/)**

Most women have never had a real menopause visit, only a menopause conversation squeezed into a visit about something else. What a thorough evaluation includes, what the MSCP credential means, and what to bring.

**[Hormone Therapy in 2026: What the Evidence Actually Says Now](/lifestyle/menopause/hormone-therapy-evidence-2026/)**

The FDA removed its boxed warning on menopausal hormone therapy in early 2026, twenty-four years after the study that put it there. What changed, what did not, and how the decision is made today.

**[Bone and Heart After Menopause: The Decade That Matters Most](/lifestyle/menopause/bone-and-heart-after-menopause/)**

The most consequential changes of menopause are the ones you cannot feel. Why bone loss and cardiovascular risk accelerate after the final period, and what the first ten years are for.
