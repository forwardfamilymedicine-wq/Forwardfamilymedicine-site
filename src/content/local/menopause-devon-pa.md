Devon residents value authenticity and quality in everything they choose, including their physicians. For the significant number of Devon women in midlife navigating perimenopause or menopause, that means finding a practice that brings real depth to this stage of life — not a brief mention at the end of a rushed appointment.

## Menopause Care at Forward Family Medicine

Menopause care isn't a separate referral at Forward Family Medicine — it's integrated into how primary care works across all three FFM offices. Devon residents have access to:

- **Primary care at our Wayne office** (about 5 minutes from Devon) — Dr. David Bigley and Dr. Philip Lieberman provide menopause care as part of comprehensive family medicine
- **Dedicated MSCP specialty consultation** with Dr. Brian Landi at our West Chester (about 25 minutes) or Malvern (about 10 minutes) offices — Menopause Society Certified, with focused training in perimenopause and menopause management

**What does Menopause Society Certified Practitioner mean?**
MSCP is a credential from the Menopause Society indicating dedicated training in perimenopause and menopause care — symptom management, HRT decisions, and long-term cardiovascular and bone health planning. Dr. Brian Landi at our West Chester and Malvern offices holds this credential.

**Is menopause care included in my membership?**
Yes — completely, across all three offices. Menopause evaluation, HRT consultation, cardiovascular and bone health monitoring, telehealth follow-ups, and inter-office coordination are all part of your DPC membership.
