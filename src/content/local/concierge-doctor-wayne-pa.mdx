<Section tone="white" label="The patient experience" title="What care looks like as a member">

### Your first visit

Plan on one to two hours, not fifteen minutes. Dr. Lieberman takes a full history, goes through your medications and prior results, and talks with you about what you want your health to look like. The approach is whole-person and prevention-first, including lifestyle medicine. He is dual board-certified in Family Medicine and Lifestyle Medicine, the only such physician at any DPC practice on the Main Line.

### Between visits

You reach Dr. Philip Lieberman, MD directly, not a call center. Follow-up is proactive: we reach out, not just the other way around. And you are talking with a physician who knows your history, so you never have to re-explain it.

### When something comes up

Same-day appointments for acute concerns, in the office or by phone or video. Visits run up to one to two hours when your situation calls for it. All of it sits under one predictable monthly fee, with no copays and no billing surprises.
